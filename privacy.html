Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

HiraLabs ("we", "us") develops the mobile application Noorly — Daily Quran & Prayer Times ("the App") and operates this website ("the Site"). This policy covers both, and explains what information each one handles.

We have written it in plain language rather than legal boilerplate, because a privacy policy you cannot read is not much of a policy.

Part one — the App

1. Summary

The App does not require an account. It does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. We do not sell data, and we do not run advertising networks inside the App.

2. Location data

The App requests access to your device's location for two features: calculating prayer times for your position, and determining the direction of the qibla.

Your coordinates are used on your device only. They are not transmitted to us, and they are not stored on any server we control.

Granting location access is optional. If you decline, you can still use the App by setting your city manually, and every Qur'an feature remains fully available. You can revoke location permission at any time through your device settings.

3. Data stored on your device

The App saves the following locally on your device, using your device's standard app storage:

  • your settings and preferences — language, theme, calculation method, notification choices
  • your bookmarks and last-read position
  • your reading history and daily practice records
  • your saved or manually entered location

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Deleting the App, or clearing its data through your device settings, removes it permanently. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had a copy.

4. Notifications

If you enable prayer time reminders, the App schedules them locally on your device. No notification content passes through our servers.

5. Third-party services used by the App

The App retrieves content from the following services. None of them receive your name, email address, or any account identifier, because the App does not have accounts. As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address.

  • Al Adhan API (aladhan.com) — prayer times for your coordinates. This is the only service that receives your location. — https://aladhan.com/privacy-policy
  • Al Quran Cloud API (alquran.cloud) — Qur'an text, translations, and recitation audio. Receives only the surah or verse number you open. — https://alquran.cloud/privacy-policy
  • Hadith API, hosted on jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — the daily hadith. Receives no information about you. — https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net
  • HUnsplash (images.unsplash.com) — background photographs shown behind the daily verse. Receives no information about you. — https://unsplash.com/privacy

Part two — the website and waitlist

6. What the waitlist collects

If you choose to join our launch waitlist, we collect:

  • your email address — required
  • which platform you intend to use — optional
  • your written answer about what you would like improved — optional

Only the email address is required. The optional questions can be left blank and the form will still submit.

7. What we do with it

If you only joined the waitlist, your email address is used for one purpose: to send you a single message on the day Noorly launches.

If you also indicated that you are willing to test an early version, see section 8 below.

We will not send you newsletters, promotions, or unrelated messages. We will not sell or rent your address, and we will not transfer it to anyone except as described in section 8.

8. If you volunteer as a tester

Before Noorly can be published, Google requires us to run a closed test with a group of testers. If you told us you were willing to take part, and we invite you, then the following applies.

We will add your email address to the tester list in Google Play Console. Google needs it to grant you access to the test build, which means your address is shared with Google for that purpose. You will receive more than one email from us: an invitation, and occasional messages during the test period. You can leave the test at any time, through the Play Store or by writing to us, and you do not need to give a reason.

If you did not volunteer, none of this applies to you and your address is never added to any tester list.

9. Where it is stored, and for how long

The waitlist is run through Google Forms, and responses are stored in Google Sheets under our account. Google acts as our data processor for this, and Google's own privacy policy applies to how they handle it: policies.google.com/privacy

After the launch announcement has been sent, we delete the response spreadsheet and close the form. Tester email addresses are removed from the Play Console tester list at the same time. Your address is not retained beyond that point.

10. Removing yourself before launch

Write to us at hello@hiralabs.net and ask to be removed. We will delete your entry and confirm that we have done so. You do not need to give a reason.

11. The website itself

This site is hosted on Squarespace. We have not added analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of our own. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as part of operating the service.

Part three — general

12. Children's privacy

Noorly is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

13. Your rights

For the App: there is no account for us to export or delete, because we hold nothing. All data stays on your device and you control it there.

For the waitlist: you may ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. Write to the address below and we will act on it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Noorly changes. When we do, we will revise the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will also announce it inside the App.
