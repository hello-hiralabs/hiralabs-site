What Noorly does

Three things, done properly.

114 surahs, with murottal audio

The complete Qur'an in clear Arabic script, with translation alongside. Play murottal recitation while you read, or listen on its own — the audio keeps going when the screen is off.

Prayer times and qibla compass

Accurate prayer times for wherever you are, with a qibla compass that works offline. Set a gentle adzan reminder, or keep it silent — the choice stays yours.

Daily practice, quietly tracked

A simple record of what you've read and when. It is there when you want to look, and it stays out of the way when you don't. Progress you can see without being pushed.

About HiraLabs

HiraLabs is a small independent software studio. We build applications that respect attention — tools you open with a purpose, use briefly, and close without feeling pulled back in.

The name is drawn from the idea of a quiet place set apart for reflection. It describes how we prefer to work, and the kind of software we want to make.

Noorly is our first product. It is free to use, and the features that matter most are not locked away. A paid tier exists to keep the studio running, never to hold something essential hostage.
